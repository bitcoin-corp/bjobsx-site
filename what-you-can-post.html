Posting a job

What you can post

Posting by other users opens soon. These are the rules it will follow.

bJobsX is for small, fixed-price contracts between people. You post the work, someone does it, and you pay them from your wallet when it's done.

Work you can post

  • Software: building, fixing or reviewing code, open source or not.
  • Design: graphics, interfaces, illustration, layout.
  • Content: writing, editing, translation, video and audio editing.
  • Research: finding and summarising information.
  • Testing your own systems: you must own them, and you say the scope in writing.
  • Token work: building, documenting, designing or running your own token or project.

Every contract needs a fixed price, a clear result and a "done when" line.

Adult work

Lawful adult work can be posted. Mark the post 18+. Everyone who appears in the work must be 18 or over and must consent. 18+ posts are hidden from anyone who isn't signed in, and they're shown only to people whose age has been checked. Until bJobsX has age checks, 18+ posts are seen only by you and our moderators.

What you can't post

  • Work that needs a licence the contractor doesn't hold, such as regulated investment advice, or legal advice as a service.
  • Paid promotion of a token or investment to the UK public, unless you say the promotion is approved by an FCA-authorised firm or is exempt. Promoting an investment without that is a crime in the UK.
  • Sexual content involving anyone under 18, or anyone who hasn't consented. We remove it and report it to the authorities.
  • Gambling, weapons or drugs, or anything illegal where you or the contractor are.
  • Testing or attacking systems you don't own.
  • Scraping, buying or selling personal data. Renting or selling accounts, identities or ID checks.
  • Fake reviews, fake followers or engagement, bots posing as people, and doing someone's coursework.
  • Ongoing jobs or recruiting. bJobsX carries contracts, not employment.
  • Any job that asks the contractor to pay anything, such as a deposit, a training fee or a "starter kit".

Paying in equity

  • Only a company can offer equity: shares or membership rights in a real company. A director, or someone the company has authorised, posts it, and we check the company number with Companies House.
  • The card says “Pay: <amount> BSV + equity in <Company Ltd>” (or “Equity in <Company Ltd>”), then “Terms are given to the chosen contractor.” It never shows a number of shares, a percentage or a value.
  • The chosen contractor sees the full terms in the private contract room, and ticks to confirm they’ve read them, before they sign.
  • A card that includes shares says: “Pay includes shares in <Company Ltd>. Terms are given to the chosen contractor. bCorp doesn’t value or endorse them.”
  • The company issues the shares itself, in its own register, after the work is signed off. bJobsX doesn’t arrange or settle it, and bCorp takes nothing from any equity.
  • Before you sign, you’ll see this warning: “Shares in a private company are high risk. You may not be able to sell them and could lose their whole value. You’ll probably owe tax on their value when you get them.”
  • A token with no legal rights behind it is token pay, not equity.

Who can post

  • You connect bWalletX.
  • You're 18 or over.
  • You give your full legal name.
  • You pass an ID check (Veriff) before your first contract goes live. It includes a sanctions check.
  • For the beta, you must live in the UK to post. Contractors can be anywhere.

Who can take a contract

  • You connect bWalletX.
  • You're 18 or over.
  • You give your full legal name when you accept.
  • Before you're paid on someone else's contract, you add your tax details in your bChatX settings. The law requires it (see the privacy notice). We never ask for them by email.

Every post is checked first

  • A new post waits for review. Nobody else sees it until a moderator approves it.
  • If you change the scope, price or pay of a live post, it goes back for review.
  • If a post can't go live, we'll tell you: "This post can't go live as written." We don't give a reason.

How pay works

  • The poster pays the contractor straight from their wallet to the contractor's wallet.
  • Pay in BSV or in any token, including your own. The card shows the amount and the token exactly as you enter them. bJobsX never says what a token is worth.
  • A card paid in a token says: “Paid in a token, not BSV. bCorp doesn’t value or endorse it.”
  • bJobsX doesn't feature, rank or endorse anyone's token or deal. Your token and your offer are yours.
  • bCorp never holds the money. There's no escrow.
  • Contractors never pay a fee. Nobody can charge you to take a contract.
  • Posting is free for now.
  • Every contract card says who it's from:
    "Posted and paid by <legal name> ($handle). bCorp runs bJobsX and isn't a party to this contract. bCorp doesn't hold the payment."
  • If the poster has passed the ID check, the card says: "Identity checked. bCorp isn't a party to this contract and doesn't guarantee payment or work."

Reporting a post or a room

  • Use Report on any contract or in any contract room.
  • Or email support@bwalletx.com with "Report" in the subject. You don't need an account.
  • Say which contract or room, and what's wrong.

When we check reports

  • Moderators work Monday to Friday, 09:00–17:30 UK time.
  • A post or room message reported outside those hours is hidden at once and stays hidden until a moderator reviews it.
  • Reports of child abuse material or terrorism are acted on at once, at any hour. We report them to the authorities (the National Crime Agency or police).

If we remove something

We can remove a post, close a room or suspend an account that breaks these rules or the law. You can appeal by emailing support@bwalletx.com with "Appeal" in the subject.
