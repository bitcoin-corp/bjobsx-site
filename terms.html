What bJobsX does

bJobsX lists small paid contracts and lets people agree and sign them. It also lets you import the data LinkedIn lets you export, show the parts you choose on a profile page, and write records of it to the Bitcoin SV blockchain in your own name. bJobsX is not part of, or endorsed by, LinkedIn.

  • You keep ownership of your content. Publishing writes the sections you choose to the blockchain, signed by you, where they stay for good. You let us show your newest published profile on your page and in Browse until you publish a newer one.

Minting and payments

  • Minting costs 1p per record plus a network fee, shown before you approve in your wallet. Payments are final.
  • A record is shown as done only once the network shows its transaction.
  • Records on a blockchain can't be changed or deleted, by you or by us. Records you make public stay public.
  • A minted record is a timestamped record of your own data. It is not a certificate from any employer or school, and it is not an investment.

What every contract says

Each contract on bJobsX is made from our template. The poster can't remove these parts:

  • both parties' full legal names;
  • that both are 18 or over;
  • the scope, deliverables, price and payment terms;
  • that bCorp isn't a party;
  • that no fee is payable by the contractor;
  • the What you can post rules;
  • the poster's promise that any promotion they commission is lawful.

Copyright and other IP: every contract says who owns the work. By default, the contractor assigns the IP to the poster once they're paid. The poster may choose a licence instead, and the contract says so.
