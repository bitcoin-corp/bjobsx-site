TN-01 · TOOLING1 BSV

Level pack format and checker

About an hour with AI. Starts the level-pack work.

Build

A data-only level pack at content/terrornode/levels/<slug>/level.json, following the bRacer pack rules in src/lib/content/schema.ts: name, blurb, Kweg's line, act, palette, erobotz mix, licence and author. Add its checker to pnpm content:check, plus one example pack. The game doesn't load packs yet.

Done when

  • The example pack passes pnpm content:check.
  • At least six bad packs fail with a clear message: bad slug, missing licence, a URL in a text field, a mix that doesn't add up to 1, an unknown palette, an act out of range. They run as tests under pnpm test.
  • pnpm typecheck and pnpm lint pass.

TN-03 · ENEMIES1 BSV

Drone swarm enemy

About an hour with AI. No other contract needed first.

Build

A new enemy: a small flock of flying erobot drones that circle and dive at the player. Pooled, so no new objects each frame, and each with a simple hit sphere so the minigun can knock them down. Off by default, switched on with ?drones=1.

Done when

  • With ?drones=1&dryrun=1, a flock appears, dives, and drones fall when shot.
  • The pool has a cap in tuning.ts, halved on the phone render tier, with a test.
  • Without ?drones=1, nothing changes. Typecheck, lint and tests pass.

How it works

  1. Claim itEmail support@bwalletx.com with the contract id in the subject and your $handle. If it's yours, we reply with the full terms; reply "I accept" before you start. One developer per contract, and we choose who. If there's no pull request after 72 hours, it opens up again.
  2. Build itFork b0ase/tokenblaster.lol, then pnpm install and pnpm dev. Add ?dryrun=1 to play free: it sends nothing and needs no keys.
  3. Open a pull requestTitle it with the id, for example TN-03: Drone swarm enemy. Say how you met each "Done when" line.
  4. Get paidWhen it's merged, 1 BSV goes to your bWalletX paymail, and the payout is listed in public on /bounties.

Getting paid

  • You need a bWalletX and an identity check with Veriff, like all our paid work. Veriff opens soon; work merged before then is paid once you're verified.
  • Payment is for merged work only. Nothing is paid for partial or unmerged work.
  • We pay only the person who passed the Veriff check, to their own paymail (yourname@bwalletx.com), never to an address pasted into a message.
  • This is paid work, not an investment. You're an independent contractor and handle your own tax.
