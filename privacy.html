Privacy

Your data stays yours.

Last updated 15 November 2026.

bJobsX (bjobsx.com) is run by THE BITCOIN CORPORATION LTD, a company registered in England and Wales, company no. 16735102 ("we"). This page says what we do with your data. We keep it short because we hold very little.

Your LinkedIn export

  • The ZIP or CSV files you choose are opened in your browser. They are never uploaded to our servers.
  • We read only Profile, Positions, Education, Skills, Certifications and Connections. From your profile we keep your name, headline, summary, industry, location and websites. We ignore your address, birth date and every other file in the export.
  • What you import is kept in your browser's storage on that device. You can remove it at any time (Import › Remove from this browser).

What is public

  • Everything is private by default.
  • When you tick sections and press Publish, your wallet signs exactly those sections and writes them to the Bitcoin SV blockchain, in clear, in one transaction you pay for (the network fee only). Your public profile is on chain, signed by you. bJobsX stores only a pointer: your username. Your page at bjobsx.com/u/your-name and Browse read it back from the chain each time.
  • A blockchain record can't be deleted. Untick and publish again (or "Make all private") and bJobsX stops showing the old sections, but earlier versions stay on chain, where anyone can still read them. Only publish what you are happy to keep public.
  • Your public profile carries your wallet's public identity key, so anyone can check it was you who signed it.

Your connections

Your connections are other people's personal data. We show them only to you, in your browser. They are never published and never sent to our server. If you tick "Include my connections", they are minted only as records locked with your own keys, which only you can open. They can't be made public on bJobsX.

Minted records

When you mint, your wallet encrypts each record with your own keys before it is written to the Bitcoin SV blockchain. Anyone can see that a record exists, its size and when it was written; only your wallet can open it. A blockchain record can't be deleted. If you choose "Make public", that one record is written in clear, signed by you, and can't be taken back.

Sign-in

You sign in by connecting bWalletX. We set one cookie to keep you signed in. It holds your wallet's public identity key and expires after 7 days or when you sign out. We look up your $name from the bWalletX paymail service. We never see your keys or your 12 words.

Linking LinkedIn

Once your wallet is connected you can link LinkedIn ("Sign in with LinkedIn"). LinkedIn tells us your name, photo, email address and whether that email is verified. It doesn't share your job history. We keep your name, your photo's link, whether your email is verified, when you linked, and a one-way hash of LinkedIn's ID for you. We don't keep your email address or any LinkedIn password or token. This is kept in a second cookie on bjobsx.com, sealed so only our server can read it and tied to your wallet. It lasts 180 days, or until you press Unlink. While you link, a third cookie lasting 10 minutes checks that the reply from LinkedIn is for you.

Payments

Minting costs 1p per record plus a small network fee. Publishing your public profile costs only the network fee (and two 1-satoshi markers that let it be found). Both are paid from your wallet, which shows the amount before you approve. We don't hold your money.

Others we use

Vercel hosts the site. Our server (push.bwalletx.com) stores usernames, never profile content. WhatsOnChain and GorillaPool are asked whether a transaction is on the network, and WhatsOnChain is where we read public profiles from the chain. WhatsOnChain and frankfurter.app give us exchange rates. Fonts load from Google Fonts and scripts from unpkg and cdnjs.

Your rights

You can see or change your public profile at any time from your profile page, and stop bJobsX showing it; we can't delete what is on chain. To ask about your data, or to have your username deleted from our server, ask b in bWalletX. If you are in the UK you can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
